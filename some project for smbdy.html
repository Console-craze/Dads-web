Services

Research that holds up when it matters.

Every engagement is scoped around a decision you need to make — not a deliverable we prefer to produce. Below is how we typically work.

01 — Research

Quantitative research

We start with the hypothesis, not the dataset. Signal discovery, factor decomposition and rigorous out-of-sample testing — designed so that a failed idea is discovered cheaply and a good one is defended convincingly.

Deliverables typically include a written research note, reproducible code and a validation report covering overfitting, survivorship and regime stability.

Signal researchFactor modelsBacktestingValidation
02 — Models

Model development

Prototypes are cheap; production models are not. We take a validated idea and build it to survive real capital — with test coverage, monitoring and clear failure modes.

Coverage includes risk models, portfolio construction, execution logic and performance attribution, implemented in your stack or ours.

Risk modelsExecutionOptimisationAttribution
03 — Data

Data infrastructure

Research is only as reproducible as the data underneath it. We build pipelines with lineage, schema contracts and observable quality checks — so a result from six months ago can be re-derived exactly.

We work with your existing warehouse and orchestration stack rather than replacing it.

PipelinesLineageData qualityReproducibility
04 — Advisory

Advisory & governance

Independent review of models, methodology and process — written for the people who have to say yes: investment committees, auditors and allocators.

Engagements are typically short and fixed-scope, and end with a written opinion rather than a slide deck.

Model reviewDocumentationDue diligence
